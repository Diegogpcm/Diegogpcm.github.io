I am a PhD student in the Department of Signal Theory and Communications at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, co-advised by Emilio Parrado-Hernández and Alejandro Lancho.

My research is on generalization in machine learning. I work towards a unified theory of generalization that combines PAC-Bayesian analysis and information theory, and I use it to study phenomena of modern deep learning such as double descent, overparameterization and the implicit regularization of SGD. I also work on non-vacuous PAC-Bayesian certificates for deep neural networks.

From March to May 2027 I will be a visiting researcher at University College London, working with John Shawe-Taylor and Benjamin Guedj.

    Advances in PAC-Bayesian certification of deep neural networks: tighter closed-form inequalities and optimization of bounds on non-differentiable losses
    Diego García-Pérez, Emilio Parrado-Hernández, and John Shawe-Taylor
    In Proceedings of the 42nd Conference on Uncertainty in Artificial Intelligence, 17–21 aug 2026
    Abs Bib HTML PDF

    This paper presents three theoretical contributions that improve the usability of risk certificates for neural networks based on PAC-Bayes bounds. First, two bounds on the KL divergence between Bernoulli distributions enable the derivation of the tightest explicit bounds on the true risk of classifiers across different ranges of empirical risk. Then, a novel method to optimize bounds on non-differentiable objectives is introduced, allowing to optimize risk bounds directly on the 0-1 loss instead of resourcing to a surrogate differentiable loss, such as the bounded cross-entropy. These theoretical contributions are illustrated with an empirical evaluation on the MNIST and CIFAR-10 datasets. In fact, this paper presents the first non data-dependent generalization bounds on the 0-1 loss for neural networks fitted on CIFAR-10.
